# Human review portal

Mode: Operate. Extend the established SpeechLens navy/teal workspace, existing fonts and native form controls. Keep the speech-analysis surface intact.

Purpose: a person can see what to do, listen, correct words, bound audible flaws, save a draft, finalize an independent file, and export it without hand-authoring JSON. The coordinator can see completion and adjudicate only after two full independent reviews. A contributor can read the chosen text, record or upload audio, consent, and withdraw it.

First viewport: a clear task brief and ordered workflow, a private reviewer identity form, and the three paths: review, annotate, contribute. Once signed in, a task ledger sits beside the listening/editing workspace. No score or target severity leaks into the independent editor.

Signature interaction: click native waveform to seek, capture start/end from the player's clock, replay a bounded event, and carry the same interval into the final schema-valid annotation file.

States: logged out, queue loading/empty, unsaved draft, saved draft, immutable submission, coordinator awaiting second review, adjudication ready/finalized, microphone permission denied, recording/uploading/received, withdrawn.

Code-first extension of the incumbent interface; no new raster assets or visual identity. Required viewports: 1440 desktop and 390 mobile. Body and controls use readable 14px+ typography; dense boundary rows scroll within their own table.
