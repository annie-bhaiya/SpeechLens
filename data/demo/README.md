# Redistributable local fixtures

`eisenhower01_good.wav` and its transcript, alignment and provenance JSON are byte-for-byte copies from the provisional training reference. The official farewell source is source-attested US federal public-domain material; recording and transcript evidence, crop clocks and original hashes are in the provenance copy. Independent human text/boundary acceptance remains pending.

The default dashboard demo chooses the shortest training reference with at least 99% machine alignment coverage, then its level-4 pacing variant. It is a deliberately reliable demonstration selection, separate from held-out evaluation.

`fdr_reference.wav` and `spike_*` preserve the initial archival compatibility experiment, including its weak alignment result. They are not certified clean alignment ground truth. This official Roosevelt speech has the same source/reuse evidence documented in the main corpus. The complete corpus and mirrors reside in the neighboring audio/manifests directories; running demo-data explicitly replaces the active manifest with a small subset.
