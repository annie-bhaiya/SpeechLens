# SpeechLens product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
SpeechLens compares same-text spoken performances through native-time acoustic evidence. Human reviewers now need guided acceptance QA, independent perceptual annotations and corrected word boundaries. A coordinator adjudicates after independent reviews. Consenting adults contribute real same-text recordings.

## Operating context
The user confirmed that reviewers will use this computer for now. React/TypeScript and FastAPI extend the existing local research workspace. The portal is private local research storage, not a public recruitment service. Dataset and YouTube publication are explicitly deferred. Terminal Git push and Docker verification are authorized.

## Capabilities and constraints
Machine supports are not perceptual labels. Independent originals must remain separate from adjudication. Blinded tasks hide generator settings and system results. Human consent and transcript identity are explicit. No human activity, approval, accuracy or public release is fabricated. Accounts are pseudonymous; the coordinator confirms that reviewers are distinct people. The existing benchmark remains frozen.

## Evidence on hand
346 provisional recordings, 13 reference excerpts, actual source provenance, machine alignments and held-out proxy measurements. The supplied three-page Track C.pdf confirms five deliverables. No real human reviews or performances have yet been supplied.

## Product principles
- Show the next action and the completion requirements in the workflow.
- Preserve original evidence and independent opinions.
- Separate listening judgments, transformation supports and predictions.
- Keep research submissions private until consent and release QA are complete.
